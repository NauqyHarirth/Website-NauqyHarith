インドネシアを拠点に活動している2Dアーティスト兼映像クリエイターです。

About Me

I am a 2D artist and visual creator based in Indonesia, specializing in motion graphics, compositing, and visual production.

My primary focus lies in creating immersive Music Videos (MV) for the Utaite community. Alongside my MV work, I have extensive professional experience as a freelance motion graphics designer and compositor for commercial advertisements and local film productions.
